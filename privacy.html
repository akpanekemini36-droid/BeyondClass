Trust & safety

Child protection and privacy.

A short summary of how we keep lessons safe and your family's information private.

Tutor screening

Every tutor, new or long standing, goes through an application review, an interview, a background check, and a demo lesson before they are matched with a family.

Session safety

Lessons happen over video call, and a parent can join or check in at any time. Tutors are trained to keep sessions focused strictly on the lesson.

Your data

Information you share with us, including your child's name, grade, and schoolwork, is used to respond to your requests and run and improve their lessons. Requests submitted through our forms are stored in our private website inbox, which only the owner can access. We do not sell family data to third parties.

We also keep anonymous page-count records containing only the page name, date, and total views. These records do not identify visitors or include form details, query strings, or referral addresses. This feature sets no tracking cookies and respects your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences.

Reporting a concern

If anything about a session feels wrong, contact us directly at hellobeyondclass@gmail.com or on WhatsApp and we will look into it right away.
