Frequently asked questions

Answers before you book.

Every tutor, new or already with us, goes through an application and credentials review, an interview, a background check, and a demo lesson before they are ever matched with a family.

GCSE, A Level, SAT prep, Common Core, and WAEC / NECO. If you are not sure which one applies, tell us the school and grade and we will work it out with you.

Your child is matched with a tutor and has a full lesson with them at no cost, so you can see how they work together before deciding on a plan.

We rematch your child with a different tutor at no extra cost. A good fit matters more than sticking with the first pairing, whether you are on a single trial lesson or a full Confidence Week.

A maximum of 4 students per week, regardless of tier, so every child gets real attention rather than being one of many.

Reaching out a few hours before your lesson guarantees a reschedule. We recommend picking lesson times that genuinely suit your schedule and keeping in touch with your assigned tutor.

A 50% part payment secures your spot once you have decided which plan suits you. We accept USD, Naira, and Pounds.

Our tutors plan around different time zones as a matter of course. Just let us know your preferred time when booking, and we will match you with a tutor who can work around it.
