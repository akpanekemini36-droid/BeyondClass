From the founder

Why BeyondClass exists.

Many kids these days spend too much time on their phones. Because of this, there is a wide spread of intelligence decline where kids know the latest brain rot, Skibidi Toilet, and what not, but cannot recite their times table.

Looking into this issue, I also found parents in the diaspora who swore, quite literally, that the math their kids were being taught abroad was way too simple for their age. After carefully diagnosing the problem and planning, we put together BeyondClass.

BeyondClass is a stand against this mental decline. In the future, there will be BeyondClass Science, Art, and specific subjects like computer science, physics, chemistry, and calculus.

Join me, either as a parent by enrolling your child, or as a skilled tutor by joining our team.

Thank you.

Ekemini Akpan

Founder, BeyondClass Math
