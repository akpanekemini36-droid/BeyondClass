Every tutor, new or long standing, goes through an application review, an interview, a background check, and a demo lesson before they are matched with a family.

Lessons happen over video call, and a parent can join or check in at any time. Tutors are trained to keep sessions focused strictly on the lesson.

Information you share with us, including your child's name, grade, and schoolwork, is used only to run and improve their lessons. We do not sell family data to third parties.
