Try it yourself: why triangle angles always add to 180°.

Drag any corner of the triangle below. Stretch it, flatten it, twist it however you like. The three angles inside will always add up to exactly 180 degrees. That is the kind of thing that clicks instantly once a child can see it move.

Drag a corner, or select one and nudge it with the arrow keys.

60° + 60° + 60° = 180°
