Know what your payment covers.

Four-week packages
Every package covers four weeks, rather than a calendar month. Essential includes 8 lessons of 60 minutes (8 hours); Growth includes 12 lessons of 90 minutes (18 hours); Ultra includes 16 lessons of 90 minutes (24 hours). We confirm your lesson dates before payment.
Progress and assessments
Essential includes a monthly progress report. Growth includes weekly parent progress updates and assessments every two months. Ultra includes weekly assessments and optional monthly parent strategy meetings.
Rescheduling
Give your assigned tutor at least two hours’ notice before the lesson starts, then agree a replacement time.
Capacity rule: quality over quantity. Each tutor is assigned a maximum of 4 students per week, regardless of tier. This keeps service quality consistent and ensures every child gets the attention their package is paying for.
