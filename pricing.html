Know what your payment covers.

Lessons included
Starter: one lesson per payment. Growth: two lessons per weekly payment. Premium: three lessons per weekly payment. Every lesson lasts 90 minutes.
Flexible’s calendar month
Three lessons per week during a calendar month, rather than a fixed four-week cycle. The total depends on the calendar and your agreed lesson dates. We confirm the billing dates and exact lesson count before you pay.
Ultra’s calendar month
Four private 90-minute lessons per week during a calendar month. The total depends on the calendar and your agreed lesson dates. We confirm the billing dates and exact lesson count before you pay.
Extra lessons
Flexible and Ultra include limited extra lessons only when the tutor decides they are needed. This is not an unlimited lesson allowance. Confirm the available extra support with your tutor before enrolling.
Progress tests
Tests take place every two months. Premium, Flexible and Ultra also include a personalized test review.
Rescheduling
Give your assigned tutor at least two hours’ notice before the lesson starts, then agree a replacement time.
Capacity rule: quality over quantity. Each tutor is assigned a maximum of 4 students per week, regardless of tier. This keeps service quality consistent and ensures every child gets the attention their package is paying for.
